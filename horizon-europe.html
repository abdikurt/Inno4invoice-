What is Horizon Europe?

The world's largest publicly funded research & innovation programme, 2021–2027.

Horizon Europe is the EU's flagship R&I programme with a €95.5 billion budget. It supports researchers, universities, companies (including SMEs), and public bodies across Europe and associated countries. The 2026–2027 Work Programme builds on earlier waves and targets the EU's Green Deal, digital transition, health, and strategic autonomy goals.

Who can apply? Universities, research institutes, industry (large & SME), public authorities, NGOs, and international organisations. Most calls require a consortium of ≥3 entities from ≥3 different EU Member States or Associated Countries. Funding rates are typically 70–100% depending on the action type.

Structure & Pillars

Three main pillars, five missions, and cross-cutting initiatives.

🔬
Pillar I — Excellent Science
~€2.1B in 2026–2027
ERC grants for frontier research, Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions (MSCA) for mobility & training, and Research Infrastructures access.
🌐
Pillar II — Global Challenges & European Industrial Competitiveness
~€7.8B in 2026–2027
Health, Culture, Digital, Industry, Space, Climate, Energy, Mobility, Food, Bioeconomy, Security. The largest pillar, with collaborative R&I projects.
🚀
Pillar III — Innovative Europe
~€2.4B in 2026–2027
European Innovation Council (EIC) Pathfinder, Transition, Accelerator. European Innovation Ecosystems. Deep-tech & scale-up funding for companies.
🌱
Mission: Climate & Oceans
Dedicated mission budget
100 climate-neutral & smart cities by 2030. Ocean, seas, coasts restoration. Soil health and food. Cancer mission. Adaptation to climate change.
🤝
Widening Participation
~€900M in 2026–2027
Teaming, Twinning, ERA Chairs, RIS3 support, and Spreading Excellence to close R&I divides across EU Member States.
🧪
European Research Council (ERC)
~€2.4B (2026–2027)
Starting Grants, Consolidator Grants, Advanced Grants, Synergy Grants, and Proof of Concept for breakthrough frontier research.
